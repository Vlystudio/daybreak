# Proposed launch-jurisdiction checklist

For every country/state where the app is offered or marketed, counsel should
record applicability, decision, responsible attorney, evidence, date, and
revalidation trigger for:

- entity qualification, consumer contract/e-sign rules, governing law, venue,
  warranty/liability language, and mandatory non-waivable rights;
- privacy controller/business identity, lawful bases/notices, rights/deadlines,
  appeals, authorized agents, sensitive-data consent, data minimization, and
  cross-border transfer requirements;
- consumer-health-data statutes, health-data authorization, sale/share and
  geofence restrictions, registration, and processor obligations;
- children/teen privacy and whether adult self-attestation is sufficient for the
  intended audience and actual-knowledge response;
- breach notification, security program, recordkeeping, retention/holds, and
  regulator/contact obligations;
- wellness/fitness/nutrition advertising, medical-device boundary, professional
  practice, emergency language, AI disclosure, and prohibited medical claims;
- automatic-renewal, subscription, tax, and payment rules as not applicable to
  free V1, with a mandatory re-review before monetization;
- email/push communications, accessibility, sanctions/export controls, contests,
  employment, insurance, and any local app-store requirements;
- trademark/name/logo/domain clearance, AI-generated output ownership,
  contractor assignments, OSS/asset licenses, and copyright notice process.

Start with the actual planned launch geography; do not infer worldwide approval
from an App Store availability setting.
