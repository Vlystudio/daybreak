# Adult-only and known-minor policy

Daybreak is intended only for adults aged 18 and older. Signup uses an unchecked
explicit checkbox and records a versioned adult self-attestation with Terms and
Privacy acceptance. This is not document, biometric, database, or third-party
age verification, and public/reviewer copy must not imply otherwise.

Pre-existing accounts are pending until the same current decision is made. A
user who indicates they are under 18 is restricted before ordinary access; AI
choices/permits are invalidated and durable deletion is queued. Credible actual
knowledge follows the authorized admin procedure in
`docs/operations/minor-account-response.md`. There is no parental-consent flow,
child mode, or conversion to a parent account. Birth-year collection was removed
and known-minor history is minimized by migration.

Counsel must decide whether self-attestation and the actual marketing/audience
are sufficient in every launch location; define credible-knowledge intake,
preservation and deletion exceptions; assess COPPA/state child-privacy rules and
Apple age-rating consistency; and approve customer communications. Engineering
must not represent the policy as independent verification.
