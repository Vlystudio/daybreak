# Support and marketing URL content

Publish these pages on the final HTTPS domain before submission. Do not use a
repository placeholder URL.

## Support page

Required content:

- Daybreak name, operator legal name, monitored support and privacy email, and
  any public phone/address required by launch law;
- response expectations and a warning not to send health details by ordinary email;
- links to password recovery, Settings privacy choices, account deletion,
  Privacy Policy, Consumer Health Privacy Policy, Terms, AI Disclosure,
  Retention, Health Disclaimer, Security, accessibility help, and status/incidents;
- device/app version, iOS version, and non-sensitive diagnostics instructions;
- account/deletion receipt help without requesting passwords, OAuth tokens,
  HealthKit exports, or other secrets;
- physical accessibility/contact alternative and copyright/security reporting.

## Marketing page

Required content:

- the same name/icon and factual free V1 feature set as App Store metadata;
- prominent “for adults aged 18 and older” and general-wellness positioning;
- account rationale; optional/read-only HealthKit and connector explanation;
- granular optional AI categories and output limitations;
- no medical, diagnostic, therapeutic, guaranteed-outcome, Apple-endorsement,
  unavailable social/subscription, or unapproved-provider claim;
- privacy, support, legal, and download links.

Test both pages without authentication, with VoiceOver/Dynamic Type, and archive
their final URLs/commit in submission evidence.
