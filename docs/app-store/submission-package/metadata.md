## Description

Daybreak is a daily wellness and planning app for adults aged 18 and older. It
brings your schedule, routines, habits, check-ins, and optional health
connections into one practical view so you can plan a day that fits real life.

Build and review your day:

- organize schedule items, routines, and habits;
- record mood, energy, stress, and wellness check-ins;
- optionally read selected Apple Health summaries and connect Oura, Fitbit, or
  Google Calendar;
- see data-source provenance when connected health sources differ;
- export your information, change permissions, disconnect providers, or delete
  your account in Settings.

Optional AI features can help draft briefings and plans. Every AI category starts
off. You choose separately whether Daybreak may use direct input, tasks,
check-ins, health context, calendar availability, calendar details, profile
preferences. You can change or withdraw those choices at any time.
AI output may be inaccurate and should be reviewed.

Daybreak provides general wellness and planning information. It is not a medical
device, diagnosis, treatment, medication, clinical, or emergency service, and it
does not guarantee outcomes. HealthKit access is optional and read-only. An
account is required to keep private data scoped to you, synchronize your choices,
secure integrations, provide export/deletion controls, and prevent cross-user
access.

The first release is free. It contains no advertising, tracking, in-app purchase,
or subscription.
