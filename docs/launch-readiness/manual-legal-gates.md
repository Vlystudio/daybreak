# Manual legal and ownership release gates

None of these gates may be checked off by software. Attach dated evidence, reviewer, scope, and expiration in `docs/launch-readiness/evidence/02-ownership-and-licenses/`; do not commit confidential agreements.

- [ ] Legal entity formation and authority to operate Daybreak.
- [ ] Apple organization Developer Program enrollment and verified seller identity.
- [ ] Paid Applications Agreement, banking, and tax setup (or documented not applicable for free V1).
- [ ] Trademark search, Daybreak name clearance, and launch-jurisdiction review.
- [ ] Logo, bird art, app icon, animation, domain, and other asset ownership evidence.
- [ ] Work-for-hire and IP assignment agreements for every contributor/vendor.
- [ ] Domain ownership and monitored privacy, security, and support mailboxes.
- [ ] Cyber-liability and technology errors-and-omissions insurance review.
- [ ] Attorney approval of every public legal document, governing law, venue, liability cap, and dispute language.
- [ ] Executed processor contracts/DPAs and completed security/retention reviews.
- [ ] OpenAI and LogMeal terms, data controls, region, DPA, training, and retention approval.
- [ ] Health-data-law applicability review, including HIPAA status without claiming compliance.
- [ ] FTC Health Breach Notification Rule assessment.
- [ ] Washington My Health My Data Act assessment.
- [ ] Nevada consumer health data law assessment.
- [ ] All other launch-jurisdiction privacy, consumer, breach, and health-data reviews.
- [ ] LGPL, MPL, FSL, and CC-BY package/license obligations reviewed with evidence.

Each checked gate requires a non-secret evidence record with reviewer, decision, date, scope, and renewal/expiration date. Absence is `BLOCKED`, never `PASS`.
