Date: September 22, 2026. Owner: Benyamin Mahmoodi.

app icon and artwork and whether they owned it or had commercial-use permission.

independent copyright, trademark or attorney opinion. The repository's original
provenance descriptions remain intact: raster birds/houses use a documented image
generation workflow, 3D birds use repository Blender scripts, and native brand
assets derive deterministically from `public/icon.svg`. Do not reinterpret the
owner's answer as claiming these were all drawn manually.

Scope: the four Daybreak-owned visual collections identified in
`config/legal/ip-assets.json`. Third-party libraries/icons retain their own license
obligations. Store screenshots require a separate capture record with synthetic
data; this statement does not approve screenshots containing private user data.
